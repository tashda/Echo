My replies to your notes

Names, ticket types and statuses

The vocabulary comes first because every screen uses it. "Round" is replaced by clearer words, and every status carries a colour for whose turn it is.

Ticket types

Question

Use it for an idea or UX issue that is still words.
You get a reply that has read the Spec and related tickets, with a recommendation.
Ends answered, often with new tickets linked.

Sketch

Use it for exploring a layout or flow before any Swift.
You get 2 to 4 variants as HTML, side by side, with pinned comments.
Ends a direction chosen. "Make it real" creates a Proposal.

Proposal

Use it for a specific change you want to see in Swift, with several options. This replaces today's Round.
You get specimens with controls and scenarios, judged against Echo today.
Ends an accepted option goes to Build.

Tweak

Use it for a small change with one obvious fix.
You get no judging step. It is checked against the Spec, built, and shown in a Preview.
Ends merged after you verify.

Bug

Use it for something in Echo that behaves wrongly. Needs steps, expected, actual.
You get one fix straight to Build, or an upgrade to a Proposal when there are several fixes.

Theme

Use it for grouping related tickets, like "Connection management".
You get a parent with progress (3 of 7 done) and its children listed.
